His story

Born in a story. Brought to life.

Dot Bot is an original Box Signal character who first emerged while we were developing a PENGU!N film. Originally imagined as a flying physical AI, Dot Bot was a small, spherical companion with two glowing white eyes and a personality all of his own.

“Dot Bot was born in a story. Then we wondered what would happen if we brought him to life.”

That idea eventually grew into Dot Bot Version 1.0, a friendly AI desktop companion available free for Windows.

From a fictional flying robot to real software, Dot Bot’s adventure is only just beginning.
