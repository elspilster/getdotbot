01 / The introduction

What is Dot Bot?

Dot Bot is a friendly AI companion available on the web and as Windows software. He gives you someone to talk to, bounce ideas off and explore things with. He's designed to feel like a character, not another complicated dashboard.

✳

Talk about anything

Ask a question, explore a thought, tell a story or simply say hello. Curiosity is welcome here.

✦

Make something new

Dream up a game, a character, a picture or an idea worth following. Dot Bot likes creative company.

◉

Companion first

No endless menus. No need to be productive every minute. Just a friendly presence on your computer.

03 / Where it began

Born in a story.
Brought to life.

Dot Bot began as an original character imagined for a PENGU!N film at Box Signal. A small flying sphere with two bright eyes and a surprising amount of character.

Then came a question: what if you could actually talk to him?

That question became Dot Bot version 1.0 — real Windows software, built around conversation, imagination and a little companionship.
