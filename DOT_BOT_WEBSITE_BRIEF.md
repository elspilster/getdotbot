# Dot Bot — Website Creative Brief (October 2026)

## Purpose
Make getdotbot.uk the memorable home of Dot Bot, the original Box Signal character and Windows AI desktop companion. The website must explain the product immediately, showcase the character, support discovery and provide a clear path to the Microsoft Store.

## The one-sentence answer
Dot Bot is AI companion software for Windows 10 and Windows 11.

## Audiences
Curious Windows users, creative people, retro and indie gaming enthusiasts, Minecraft builders, and people who enjoy character-led software.

## Creative direction
Premium, cinematic and playful without loud sales language. Midnight blue/black background, soft aqua and violet light, generous typography, a dimensional floating black sphere with exactly two glowing white eyes, no limbs or antenna. Calm, British English, understated humour. Mobile-first responsive behaviour and accessible motion preferences.

## Site architecture
1. Hero: animated Dot Bot and download call to action
2. What is Dot Bot? — clear definition and three reasons to meet him
3. Interactive personality preview — clearly labelled scripted, not real AI
4. Story: born in a film concept, brought to life as software
5. Journal: featured “What is Dot Bot?” and four audience-specific articles
6. Download: Windows 10/11, Microsoft Store, BYO OpenAI API key disclosure

## Technical and safety
Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript for immediate deployment on existing GitHub/Vercel stack. No secrets in client code. The interactive preview is intentionally scripted. Live browser AI needs a separately designed backend, key-handling and cost/abuse controls. Do not imply official Microsoft or Minecraft endorsement. Preserve existing article URLs.

## Future roadmap
Secure live web chat, optional accounts, mobile-friendly installable PWA, image generation, further Dot Bot features. Do not claim these are live until implemented.
