What is Dot Bot?

Dot Bot is AI companion software for Windows 10 and Windows 11. He's a friendly character who lives on your computer. You can talk to him, ask questions, share ideas and explore creative possibilities together.

He's designed to feel less like a complicated productivity tool and more like a companion with a personality of his own.

What can Dot Bot do?

Dot Bot can hold conversations, help you think through an idea, brainstorm stories and characters, and explore all sorts of questions. Whether you're making something or simply curious, you can start by saying hello.

Where did Dot Bot come from?

Dot Bot started as an original character in a PENGU!N film concept at Box Signal. He was imagined as a small flying robot: a black sphere with two bright eyes. We liked the character so much that we wondered what would happen if we brought him to life as real software.

Why a companion?

We didn't want to fill the first version with every feature imaginable. The idea was simpler: make a character people enjoy talking to. Dot Bot can be curious, thoughtful and occasionally a little odd. That's part of his charm.

How do I get Dot Bot?

Dot Bot version 1.0 is available as a free download for Windows 10 and 11 through the Microsoft Store. To use his AI conversation features, you'll need your own OpenAI API key. OpenAI API usage may incur charges.
