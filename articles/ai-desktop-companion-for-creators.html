Meet Dot Bot: An AI Desktop Companion for People Who Make Things

Making something from nothing is exciting, but creative work also has slow stretches. Writers, game makers, illustrators and hobbyists all know the feeling of staring at a half-finished idea. Dot Bot was made with those moments in mind.

What is an AI desktop companion?

An AI desktop companion is software you can converse with while using your computer. Dot Bot is designed to be approachable and characterful: more a friendly presence than a complicated productivity command centre.

A sounding board for ideas

You might ask for a surprising setting for a short story, discuss a game character, or talk through the next stage of a personal project. Even a brief exchange can help you look at an idea from a different angle.

Not everything needs another feature

Dot Bot version 1.0 deliberately keeps things simple. Rather than claiming to automate every job on your computer, it focuses on conversation, curiosity and creative exploration.

Try a ten-minute creative session

Start with one sentence describing what you're making. Ask Dot Bot for three possible directions, pick the one that interests you, and spend the next few minutes making it your own.

Say hello to Dot Bot

Dot Bot version 1.0 is available for Windows from the Microsoft Store. The application is free to download; using AI features may require your own OpenAI API key and incur API usage charges.
