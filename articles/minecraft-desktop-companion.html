A Desktop Companion for Minecraft Builders: Ideas, Stories and Company

Minecraft is a place to build, explore and experiment. But even the most enthusiastic builder sometimes runs out of ideas. A separate desktop companion can make a long creative session feel a little less solitary.

When your next build needs a spark

Try giving your next world a theme: a harbour town built into cliffs, a railway that connects abandoned stations, or a castle with a market below its walls. Rather than asking for a complete blueprint, start with a small prompt and let the idea grow as you play.

A companion beside the game

Dot Bot is a friendly Windows AI desktop companion. You can talk to him about a story, invent characters for your world, or brainstorm what to build next. He runs as a separate application; he does not enter your Minecraft world, control your game, or add blocks and mods.

Make your world tell a story

A village becomes more memorable when it has a reason to exist. Who built its clock tower? Why is the old bridge broken? What lives beyond the forest? A conversation can turn a simple building session into an imaginative adventure.

Keep the creativity yours

The best part of a sandbox game is deciding what happens next. Dot Bot is there to keep you company and offer ideas, not to replace the joy of discovering things yourself.
